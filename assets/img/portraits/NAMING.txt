Portrait files go here. The filenames must match the STAGES list in
assets/js/portrait.js exactly:

  undergrad.jpg
  mphil.jpg
  phd-start.jpg
  phd-end.jpg
  postdoc.jpg
  ap-start.jpg
  ap-now.jpg

The files currently in this folder are grey placeholders so the stepper
works out of the box. Overwrite them with your photos, same filenames.

Preparation, in order of how much it matters:

1. Crop every photo to 4:5 (portrait). The CSS frame is locked to that
   ratio, so anything else gets cropped by the browser and you lose
   control of the framing.

2. Put your eyes at roughly the same height in every frame, about a third
   of the way down, and keep your head about the same size. This is the
   whole trick. Consistent framing makes the sequence read as one person
   ageing. Inconsistent framing makes it read as a shoebox of snapshots.

3. Export at 800x1000 pixels, JPEG quality 80. That is plenty for a
   150px-wide display slot on a retina screen, and keeps the total
   payload of all seven photos under about 400 KB.

4. Convert to greyscale if the colour grading varies wildly between a
   2011 phone camera and a 2026 one. Optional, but it hides a multitude
   of sins and looks deliberate.

Free tools that do all of this: GIMP, Photopea (browser, no install), or
ImageMagick from the command line:

  magick input.jpg -resize 800x1000^ -gravity center -extent 800x1000 \
    -quality 80 undergrad.jpg
